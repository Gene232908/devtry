Before you start

Use this as a guide, not a word-for-word reading. Look at the screen, explain the reason behind the feature, then give one example. Keep your pace calm and conversational.
SAY THIS - opening

Good day. I will walk you through the admin side of CarVibe. I will start with the overview, then I will follow the actual business flow: a valuation becomes a lead, a deal becomes inventory, and a sold car becomes a commission record. I will also show the support and configuration pages after that.

SHOW / CLICK

Start at /admin. Point out the left navigation: Dashboard, Leads, Inventory, Commission, Incomplete Forms, Blocked Numbers, Vehicle Catalog, and Settings.

LIKELY BASH / QUESTION

If someone says, 'Can you just click everything quickly?' do not rush. Say: 'I will keep it short, but I want to show the connection between pages because that is where the business value is.'

ANSWER / PROOF

The sidebar order follows the real process: leads come in, a deal creates stock, stock is sold, and the result appears in commission. The other pages protect and maintain that flow.

1. Login and access

Purpose: Only authenticated admin users should enter the private dashboard.

SAY THIS - natural speaking line

The first screen is the admin login. I enter the authorized account, and after authentication the system takes me into the protected dashboard. The public website and the admin dashboard are separate areas.

SHOW / CLICK

Show /admin/login only if requested. Otherwise mention that the session is authenticated and open the Overview.

LIKELY BASH / QUESTION

Is this only hidden by the URL?

ANSWER / PROOF

No. The admin routes and admin API requests are protected by server-side session checks. The browser is not treated as the authority.

2. Overview / Dashboard

Purpose: Give management a quick picture of pipeline activity, customer interest, and recent leads.

SAY THIS - natural speaking line

This is the first page I use for a quick health check. The top cards show how many leads are in each stage - new, pending, contacted, inspection scheduled, inspected, negotiating, deal, and closed. I can see the overall movement without opening every record.

SHOW / CLICK

Point to the pipeline cards, Message Us and Call Now clicks, Incomplete Forms, Visitors today, the two charts, and Recent Leads. Click Refresh once to show that the view can be refreshed.

LIKELY BASH / QUESTION

Are these numbers hardcoded?

ANSWER / PROOF

The dashboard is wired to the admin data layer. The current environment may contain test or demo records, so I would label those clearly during a client review. The page also keeps incomplete forms separate from the main lead pipeline so the numbers are not mixed.

3. Leads Directory

Purpose: Search, filter, export, and follow up on every valuation request.

SAY THIS - natural speaking line

This is the working list of valuation requests. I can search by customer or vehicle, filter by status, and open a customer record for the next action. The point is to make follow-up easy and visible instead of keeping it in messages or memory.

SHOW / CLICK

Show the table, search box, status filter, pagination, and CSV export. Open a sample lead such as Noura Al Hammadi or another clearly marked test record.

LIKELY BASH / QUESTION

What happens when there are many leads?

ANSWER / PROOF

The current list has a server-side cap as a safety guard, and browser pagination keeps the view manageable. True server-side pagination is still a follow-up before the lead volume becomes large.

Demo leads and Activity history

Purpose: Prove the filters and show a clear follow-up history.

SAY THIS - natural speaking line

I click Create Demo Leads once, then test status, make, and emirate filters. The records are clearly marked as demonstration data and exist only on Leads. On a lead detail page, the Follow-up note shows the latest working note. When an assigned CSR, Purchaser, or Super Admin saves it, Activity keeps a dated copy with the staff member and role who saved it. The Activity card shows the five newest actions first.

SHOW / CLICK

Create the demo batch, try New and Pending, choose a make and emirate, then open one lead. Type a safe note such as 'Called seller, inspection confirmed for Friday,' and click Save changes. Point to the Activity entry with the note, date/time, and staff member. Change the status and save once more. If there are more than five activities, click See full details. Show that the full timeline opens in a popup and scrolls inside the popup after roughly ten visible entries. After the meeting, click Delete Demo Leads. Cleanup removes commission entries and inventory dependencies first, then the demo leads, so foreign-key errors are avoided.

LIKELY BASH / QUESTION

Does a new note overwrite the old one completely?

ANSWER / PROOF

The box shows the latest working note for the next action. Activity keeps every saved version with the date, time, and staff member, so the follow-up trail is not lost.

5. Inventory

Purpose: Track cars the business has bought, their current status, and the money tied up in stock.

SAY THIS - natural speaking line

This page represents cars the business owns. A car arrives here from a lead marked as Deal. I can filter by status and purchase month, see the purchase price, and follow the link back to the original customer record.

SHOW / CLICK

Show the current-month filter, status filter, summary cards, and a sample in-stock or reserved car. Then open the sale dialog for a demo car, but do not submit it unless the dataset is specifically for the demo.

LIKELY BASH / QUESTION

Can someone add a car directly here?

ANSWER / PROOF

No, by design. Inventory must have a lead and a purchase price behind it. That keeps the money trail connected to the customer record. A sold car also freezes the purchase basis used for commission.

Inventory overview: do not confuse these three cards

Purpose: The three cards use the same unsold cars, but each answers a different business question.

SAY THIS - natural speaking line

Money in Cars for Sale is the purchase money currently tied up in every car that is still unsold. Average Time in Stock is the average age in days of those same unsold cars, counted from each purchase date until today. Cars Waiting Too Long is only the count of unsold cars that are over 90 days old. So the first is an AED amount, the second is an average number of days, and the third is a warning count of vehicles.

SHOW / CLICK

Point from left to right. Use this simple example: we have two unsold cars bought for AED 100,000 and AED 150,000. Money in Cars for Sale is AED 250,000. If those cars have been in stock for 20 and 40 days, Average Time in Stock is 30 days. If one car reaches 95 days, Cars Waiting Too Long becomes 1. Say: AED 250,000 is money tied up, 30 days is the usual current stock age, and 1 is the car that needs attention now.

LIKELY BASH / QUESTION

Are Money in Cars for Sale and Cars Waiting Too Long the same?

ANSWER / PROOF

No. Money in Cars for Sale includes every unsold car, even one bought yesterday. Cars Waiting Too Long includes only unsold cars over 90 days. Average Time in Stock is not money and not a warning count; it is the average age of the current unsold stock. These cards are not limited by the Dashboard date filter because they describe what is in stock right now.

6. Commission

Purpose: Turn completed sales into a clear monthly and quarterly payment view.

SAY THIS - natural speaking line

When an inventory car is sold for more than its purchase price, the system calculates two percent of the profit. For example, if we buy at AED 170,000 and sell at AED 185,000, the profit is AED 15,000 and the commission is AED 300. If a car is sold at a loss, the commission is zero. The month is based on the date the car was sold, not the date it was purchased. So a car bought in August but sold in September belongs to the September statement. Open or refresh the page and the current open month is rebuilt automatically.

SHOW / CLICK

Show the quarter cards, expand a quarter to show monthly statements, and point out Draft, Issued, and Paid statuses. Explain that the record is monthly but payment is settled every three months. Show the statement export button if available.

LIKELY BASH / QUESTION

Why is there no commission on a loss, and what happens if a sale is late or backdated?

ANSWER / PROOF

The rule is profit-based. A loss should not create a positive commission. Open months update automatically from sales. Once a quarter is issued or paid, it is locked; a late or backdated sale goes to the next open month as carried forward so it is not lost or added to a settled statement.

7. Incomplete Forms

Purpose: Recover people who started the valuation form but did not finish.

SAY THIS - natural speaking line

This is not yet a real lead. The person may have entered a name or number, but the details are not complete or confirmed yet. So the system gives the record to a Call Centre agent first, not directly to a Purchaser.

SHOW / CLICK

Open one record. First point to the Call Centre assignment, then the contact buttons, notes, status, and activity history. Explain that the CSR calls the person, confirms the name, car details, emirate, and inspection schedule.

LIKELY BASH / QUESTION

When does this become a real lead, and who handles it next?

ANSWER / PROOF

Once the CSR has spoken to the customer and completed the missing details, they choose 'Convert to Lead & Schedule Inspection.' At that moment, the system creates one real lead and automatically assigns a Purchaser. The Purchaser only sees it after it is a proper inspection booking. Example: Anna leaves her number, the CSR confirms her 2022 BMW in Dubai and books tomorrow at 10 AM, then the system creates Anna's lead for the Purchaser.

8. Blocked Numbers

Purpose: Prevent spam or OTP abuse from repeatedly reaching the business or consuming message capacity.

SAY THIS - natural speaking line

This page shows numbers already blocked, the reason, and suspicious numbers that recently triggered automatic limits. Suspicious does not automatically mean blocked, because a real customer can also hit a limit by mistake.

SHOW / CLICK

Show the block reason labels, suspicious numbers, and the confirmation dialog before blocking or unblocking. Do not change a real number during the demo.

LIKELY BASH / QUESTION

Does the system automatically block everyone suspicious?

ANSWER / PROOF

No. Automatic guards surface suspicious behavior, while a human confirmation is used for the final block or unblock action. The customer-facing response stays generic so the system does not reveal which security rule was triggered.

9. Vehicle Catalog

Purpose: Control the make, model, and trim options shown in the public valuation form.

SAY THIS - natural speaking line

This is where the admin maintains the vehicle list. I select a make, expand a model, and add or remove trims. Whatever is managed here becomes the catalog visitors can use in the valuation flow.

SHOW / CLICK

Search for a make or model, select a make, expand a model, and point out the add-model and add-trim controls. Explain the confirmation step before deletes because deletion can cascade.

LIKELY BASH / QUESTION

If I add a model here, will customers see it?

ANSWER / PROOF

Yes, the catalog is the shared source for the public valuation dropdowns. The page refreshes the list and the API validates the change before it is saved.

10. Settings

Purpose: Maintain the business contact details and notification destination used by the system.

SAY THIS - natural speaking line

Here I can update the WhatsApp number, call number, and notification email. These values power the public contact buttons and lead notification behavior. The page also shows the staff accounts that can sign in.

SHOW / CLICK

Show the three settings areas, edit a field only if you have permission and a safe value, then show the Save changes state. Point out that manager accounts can read but only admins can edit.

LIKELY BASH / QUESTION

Does clicking Save always mean it was saved?

ANSWER / PROOF

The success message appears only after the server confirms the database update. If validation or the network fails, the form keeps the changes and shows an error instead of pretending the save worked.

Closing script

SAY THIS - closing

So that is the complete admin workflow. The dashboard gives the overview, Leads handles follow-up, Customer Details keeps the conversation and audit trail, Deal creates Inventory, and a completed sale rolls into Commission. Incomplete Forms, Blocked Numbers, Vehicle Catalog, and Settings support the main process around it.

SAY THIS - strong final point

The main thing I want to highlight is that the pages are connected. The admin does not need to retype the same vehicle in multiple places, and the financial figures can be traced back to the original lead and sale.

LIKELY BASH / QUESTION

If they ask, 'What is still needed before production?' say this: 'The workflow is implemented, but we still need final client approval for policies such as data retention, the final production acceptance test, and any remaining legal or operational requirements. I would not call those approved until they are verified.'

ANSWER / PROOF

Strong final point: demonstrate the relationship, not just the design. One lead, one deal, one inventory car, one sale, and one commission statement is the story they should remember.

Quick recovery lines if you forget

If the page is slow'I will give it one moment - this screen is loading the current server data.'
If a demo row is missing'That record is not in this dataset, so I will use the next safe sample. The workflow is the same.'
If they ask about a future feature'That is a good next scope item. The current page is intentionally limited to the workflow shown here.'
If you lose your place'Let me connect this back to the business flow: lead, deal, inventory, sale, commission.'

Tip: speak in short chunks. Explain what the page is for, show one example, then move on. The audience should feel that you understand the workflow, not that you memorized every label.
