## Alignment With The Signed Proposal

This milestone plan follows the signed CarVibeDubai proposal and the technical build plan. It does not replace the signed proposal, change the agreed PHP 67,000 professional development fee, or expand the signed scope.

The approved project focus is:

- Public CarVibeDubai website for car-seller lead capture.
- Car submission form with Make, Model, Year, Mileage, and Condition.
- Phone OTP verification through Twilio before submission.
- Thank-you confirmation with no automatic price shown to the customer.
- Private admin dashboard for authorized CarVibeDubai Management users.
- CRM pipeline for tracking leads through sale or not-purchased stages.
- Gmail notification when a new lead is submitted.
- Completed-sale record with purchase price, final selling price, profit, and completion date.
- Automatic 2% developer partnership-share calculation from completed-sale profit records.
- Settings for contact details, Gmail address, pipeline stages, and future team member accounts.
- Disabled "Buy a Car" button prepared for future activation.
- Launch preparation, testing, handover, and 30-day technical warranty for original build defects.

## Timeline Note

The 45-day timeline is a realistic estimate. Some parts may finish earlier if all details, assets, access, and feedback are ready on time. If the project is waiting for client information, approval, payment, domain access, Supabase access, Twilio setup, Gmail access, Meta Pixel details, or other required accounts, the timeline may extend accordingly.

The plan should begin after:

- The finalized schedule is confirmed through the agreed written communication channel.
- Required information and access are provided.
- The first scheduled payment is received, based on the finalized payment milestone arrangement.

## Milestone 1 - Public Website And Landing Page

Estimated schedule: Days 1 to 15

Main goal: Prepare the customer-facing CarVibeDubai website so it looks premium, works well on mobile, and clearly leads visitors toward submitting their car details.

Planned work:

- Build the public website using original code, not a pre-made template.
- Prepare the premium black and gold landing page direction.
- Add the main headline, supporting message, and call-to-action.
- Prepare the navigation for the main sections.
- Add the How It Works section.
- Add the valuation entry area for customers.
- Add the floating WhatsApp chat and call access.
- Prepare the disabled "Buy a Car" button as agreed.
- Add the contact section and footer, including the approved developer credit line.
- Make the page responsive for phones, tablets, and desktop screens.
- Review and adjust wording, spacing, and layout based on feedback.

Expected result by the end of this milestone:

- A premium public website direction is ready for review.
- Customers can understand what CarVibeDubai offers and where to submit their car details.
- The public page is mobile-friendly and aligned with the signed black/gold direction.
- Remaining revisions should mostly be wording, images, spacing, or brand-detail adjustments.

Client input needed:

- Final logo file.
- Final brand name spelling.
- Preferred headline and short message.
- WhatsApp number, call number, and public contact details.
- Approval of footer credit wording.
- Any final design reference or Canva notes.

## Milestone 2 - Valuation Form, OTP, And Database Foundation

Estimated schedule: Days 16 to 25

Main goal: Prepare the customer submission flow so real leads can be validated, verified, and stored properly.

Planned work:

- Build the car submission form.
- Add dropdowns for Make, Model, Year, Mileage, and Condition.
- Add customer name and phone number fields.
- Add validation for required fields and incorrect entries.
- Prepare phone OTP verification through Twilio.
- Ensure only confirmed phone numbers can submit.
- Add the thank-you confirmation message after submission.
- Keep the customer flow clear that no automatic price or valuation is shown at this stage.
- Set up the Supabase database foundation under the CarVibeDubai Management account.
- Prepare lead records, customer contact data, car details, CRM activity, and completed-sale data structure.
- Prepare basic security rules so public visitors cannot access private lead data.
- Prepare the data flow for the admin dashboard and CRM pipeline.

Expected result by the end of this milestone:

- The public form is ready to collect customer details.
- Phone verification and lead capture are prepared according to the signed scope.
- The database structure is ready for the private dashboard and CRM tracking.

Client input needed:

- Final car dropdown lists.
- Final form field order.
- Final thank-you message.
- Twilio setup or access details.
- Supabase account ownership/access details.
- Confirmation of any optional fields such as location, preferred contact time, or car photos.

## Milestone 3 - Private Admin Dashboard And CRM Pipeline

Estimated schedule: Days 26 to 35

Main goal: Build the private admin area where CarVibeDubai Management can receive, review, follow up, and track leads through the sales process.

Planned work:

- Build the private admin dashboard.
- Add secure OTP login for authorized accounts.
- Add dashboard summary cards for total submissions, new leads, visitor count, pipeline status, and completed transactions.
- Build the leads directory with customer name, phone number, car details, submission date, current stage, and notes.
- Build the lead detail view with complete information.
- Add one-click WhatsApp contact from lead records.
- Add follow-up notes.
- Add status history so authorized users can see how a lead moved through the pipeline.
- Add the CRM stages from the signed proposal:
  - New Lead.
  - Contacted.
  - Inspection Scheduled.
  - Inspected.
  - Under Negotiation.
  - Completed Sale.
  - Not Purchased.
- Add completed-sale fields:
  - Vehicle purchase price.
  - Final selling price.
  - Calculated profit.
  - Transaction completion date.
- Add automatic 2% partnership-share calculation based on completed-sale profit records.
- Prepare monthly partnership-share records and quarterly settlement summary.

Expected result by the end of this milestone:

- CarVibeDubai Management has a private working dashboard.
- Leads can be reviewed, contacted, updated, and tracked through the approved CRM pipeline.
- Completed sales can be recorded with profit and partnership-share calculation.

Client input needed:

- Authorized admin email or login details.
- Confirmation of who can access the dashboard.
- Final pipeline stage wording if any labels need adjustment.
- Confirmation of completed-sale data entry responsibilities.

## Out-Of-Scope Reminder

The signed proposal excludes the following unless separately agreed in writing:

- Full buy-and-sell marketplace.
- Arabic or bilingual language work.
- Identity verification / KYC.
- Payment gateway.
- Advanced SEO package.
- Mobile app.
- Complete redesigns after approval.
- Major system enhancements or new modules.
- Social media posting.
- Ad account or campaign management.
- Routine ad analytics checking or reporting.
- Google Ads handling.
- Original artwork, poster, photo, video, voiceover, or promotional creative production.
- On-site work, relocation, travel, or physical presence in the UAE.

The "Buy a Car" button is included only as a disabled feature that can be enabled later when CarVibeDubai is ready. A full marketplace remains a separate future phase.

## Third-Party Cost Reminder

The project is planned around free-plan usage where possible, but third-party accounts and usage costs remain owned and paid directly by CarVibeDubai Management:

- Domain registration and renewal.
- Vercel hosting, initially intended on the free plan unless an upgrade becomes required.
- Supabase database, initially intended on the free plan unless usage requires an upgrade.
- Twilio OTP usage-based top-up.
- Gmail account used for notifications, subject to Gmail limits.
- Meta Business Manager and Meta Pixel account access, if tracking is used.
